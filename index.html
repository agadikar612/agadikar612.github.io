Classic Chewy Chocolate Chip Cookies

Golden, crisp edges with soft, gooey centers and pockets of melted dark chocolate.

Prep Time 15 mins
Bake Time 10 mins
Yield 24 cookies
Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. 1

    Preheat and Prepare

    Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Line two large baking sheets with parchment paper or silicone baking mats.

  2. 2

    Combine Dry Ingredients

    In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, and sea salt until combined. Set aside.

  3. 3

    Cream Butter and Sugars

    In a large bowl, beat the softened butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar with a mixer on medium speed for about 2 minutes until creamy and smooth.

  4. 4

    Add Eggs and Vanilla

    Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition. Stir in the vanilla extract.

  5. 5

    Mix Dry into Wet & Add Chocolate

    Gradually beat in the flour mixture on low speed until just combined. Fold in the chocolate chips with a spatula.

  6. 6

    Scoop and Bake

    Drop rounded tablespoons of dough onto prepared baking sheets, spaced 2 inches apart. Bake for 9 to 11 minutes until golden brown around the edges. Cool on baking sheet for 2 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack.

💡 Baker's Tip: Chill the cookie dough in the refrigerator for at least 1 hour before baking to prevent spreading and deepen the flavor!
